# AI Assistant Product Goal

The goal is to build an AI assistant integrable into consulting businesses as a true ally to employees.

It should:
- Present and explain documents clearly during team meetings.
- Support brainstorming and convert conversations into actionable ideas.
- Listen to what is happening in real time and assist the team as an ultimate operational asset.

Long-term direction:
- Adapt behavior to each industry and mission context.
- Acquire any skill needed for each specific role and workflow.
- Operate with human-like execution quality while targeting a radically lower operating cost.
